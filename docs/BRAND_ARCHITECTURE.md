# SquareBoards Brand Architecture

> **Executive Product Directive #001 — Project Legacy**  
> Full product direction: `docs/PROJECT_LEGACY.md`

## Strategic shift

SquareBoards is **the platform** — not a single game. Sports Squares is one experience inside it.

We are not marketing a sports squares website. We are building **the premium sports gaming platform**.

Think: Xbox → Halo, Steam → Counter-Strike, Netflix → Stranger Things. The **platform** is the destination; games are experiences inside it.

---

## Brand hierarchy

```
SquareBoards (platform)
├── Sports Squares (flagship — NFL)

├── MLB Pick'em / MLB Squares
├── Soccer Predictor
```

**Ecosystem pillars** (not separate products):

| Pillar | Role |
|--------|------|

## Product philosophy

A new visitor should think: *"I've discovered an entire sports gaming platform."*

Not: *"I found a website that sells sports squares."*

- The **platform** is the product.
- The **games** are the experiences.
- **Rewards** are progression.
- **Legacy** is identity.
- **The Huddle** is community.
- **Square Drop** is the engagement engine.

---

## Design mindset

Think like Apple, Xbox, PlayStation, or Steam — not a generic marketing site.

Every screen should answer: *"What makes someone excited to stay inside the SquareBoards ecosystem?"*

- **Homepage** → sell the platform first, then showcase games.
- **Game pages** → sell that experience after the user is inside the platform.

---

## Copy guidelines

### Do

- Lead with **SquareBoards** and platform language ("Browse games", "One account, every experience").
- Name games as **experiences inside the platform** (e.g. "Sports Squares on SquareBoards").
- Use **The Premium Sports Gaming Platform** as the category positioning.

### Don't

- Call SquareBoards a "sports squares website" or "squares marketplace" in primary copy.
- Lead hero/meta/footer with squares-only messaging.
- Treat Pick'em, Huddle, or rewards as afterthoughts — they are core ecosystem.

---

## Launch goal (v1.0)

SquareBoards should be recognized as **the premium sports gaming platform** — not simply the best sports squares website.
